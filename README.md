**7ARRB EL KATKOT**

7ARRB EL KATKOT is a fast-paced, arcade-style shooter that throws players into a chaotic and humorous battlefield filled with relentless waves of quirky enemies. Designed with simplicity and fun at its core, the game challenges players to survive as long as possible while mastering movement, timing, and precision.

The gameplay revolves around quick reflexes and constant action, where every moment matters. As the difficulty progressively increases, players must adapt their strategies, improve their aim, and stay focused to overcome increasingly intense encounters. Despite its simple controls and accessible design, the game offers a satisfying challenge for those who seek to push their limits and achieve high scores.

Visually, the game embraces a lighthearted and cartoon-inspired style, giving it a unique personality and charm. Combined with engaging gameplay mechanics, this creates an experience that is both entertaining and memorable.

7ARRB EL KATKOT is more than just a game — it is a passionate project developed by university students who are exploring game development, design principles, and creative expression. It reflects a dedication to learning, experimentation, and building something enjoyable from the ground up.

**Features:**

* Fast-paced arcade gameplay focused on survival and skill
* Gradually increasing difficulty to keep players engaged
* Simple and intuitive controls suitable for all players
* Unique, humorous visual style and theme
* Score-based progression encouraging replayability

**Project Purpose:**
This project is part of a student-driven journey into game development, aiming to apply practical knowledge, experiment with ideas, and create a fun experience for players while learning along the way.
